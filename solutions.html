Workflow 01

Biomarker Strategy

Identify, prioritize, and contextualize biomarkers based on disease biology, clinical relevance, competitive landscape, assay feasibility, and development value.

Includes

  • Biomarker prioritization
  • Disease-specific biomarker landscape
  • Mechanism-of-action alignment
  • Patient segmentation and translational rationale

Example outputs

  • Evidence summary
  • Biological rationale
  • Strategy recommendation

Biomarker-Based Clinical Trial Planning

Support translational and clinical teams in planning biomarker-informed development strategies across patient enrichment, evidence, feasibility, and competitive dynamics.

Includes

  • Patient enrichment rationale
  • Biomarker-informed inclusion and exclusion
  • Trial landscape and competitor benchmarking
  • Recruitment and prevalence feasibility

Example outputs

  • Trial planning summary
  • Competitor benchmark
  • Assay and testing considerations
  • Development-stage recommendation

  • Cutoff and sample-type risk flags
